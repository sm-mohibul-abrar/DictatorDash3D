How to play

Run the royal road. Dodge what you can't shoot, shoot what you can. Every 400 m the guards carry a finish tape out to you: break it for a gold medal.

  • Rivals in tracksuits jog ahead of you. Fire and they freeze with their hands up. Shoot them to disqualify them, or go around.
  • Democracy is the enemy: robots with placards (VOTE, DEMOCRACY, FREEDOM, EQUAL RIGHTS) march at you and shoot, drones fire ballot papers, and ballot boxes block the lanes.
  • Stamina: running faster than cruising pace (the speed buttons, Q / E) drains it. Empty, and he is exhausted for a few seconds.
  • Barriers: jump. Beams: slide. Crates: shoot or dodge.
  • Coins, kills and medals build a combo up to x5. Getting hit resets it.
  • Pick-ups: VIP pass (blocks one hit), rapid fire, health, and rocket boots (a burst of unstoppable speed).
  • Score comes from what you do (coins, medals, enemies, disqualified rivals), never from just running.
  • Coins you bank unlock new Athletes and buy gear at Nuclear Nadal's Lab. The Pointy Hand Missile hunts the nearest enemy and reloads on a timer. The Pointy Beard of Doom charges on kills and medals, then clears the whole track. The Coin Magnet pulls coins to you.
